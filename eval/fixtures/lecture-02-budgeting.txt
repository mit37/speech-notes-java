Today we are covering personal budgets. First, write down every fixed cost, because rent and insurance do not care about your intentions. Second, separate savings from spending on the day the salary arrives, not at the end of the month. Third, track one variable category properly instead of five badly. An emergency fund of three months of expenses is the usual target, and six months if your income is irregular. Action: open a separate savings account and move the deposit to payday. Finally, a budget is a plan you revise, not a score you lose.
