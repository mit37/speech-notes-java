Let us prepare for a technical interview. First, restate the problem in your own words, because half of the wrong answers are answers to a different question. Second, describe the simplest approach that works, then improve it out loud. Third, state the complexity before you write code, not after. If you get stuck, name the part that is blocking you instead of going quiet for a minute. Action: practise three problems aloud this week and record yourself. Finally, an interview is a conversation about trade offs, not a memory test.
