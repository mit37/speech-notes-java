This lecture is about caching in web systems. First, a cache only pays off when the same data is requested more than once. Second, every cached entry needs an expiry rule, otherwise stale data outlives the bug that created it. Third, measure the hit rate before you add another layer, because two weak caches are still weak. In our experiment a one minute expiry reached eighty four percent, and a five minute expiry reached ninety one percent. Action: instrument the hit rate on the product page this week. Finally, remember that a cache is a bet about the future, not a copy of the truth.
